How I Built My Wardrobe at NIFT Through Swaps

By Ananya Sen • Textile Design (TD '26)

"Coming to NIFT Hyderabad, you quickly realise how much money students spend on styling assignments and runway looks that only get worn once. Instead of letting beautiful garments gather dust in hostel lockers, we started trading in the Canteen courtyard during lunch breaks. Last semester, I traded my handloom Chanderi blouse for a vintage biker jacket and denim trousers. It’s not just circular fashion—it’s how our community connects across departments."
